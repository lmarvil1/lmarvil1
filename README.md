## Featured Projects

### Employee Time Tracking Application
**Final Degree Project | Flutter · Dart · Firebase**

Development of a multiplatform employee time tracking application designed for real business use.

The application includes:
- Employee attendance and working hours tracking
- Vacation and absence management
- Incident reporting and supporting documents
- Role-based access and administration
- PDF and CSV report generation

The application has been implemented in a real company and is actively used by its employees.

### Delivery Notes Management Application
**Independent Project | Flutter · Firebase · Google Drive**

Development of a digital solution to simplify the creation and management of delivery notes.

Main features:
- Creation and management of digital delivery notes
- Electronic signatures and photo attachments
- PDF document generation
- Google Drive integration
- Administrative dashboard with filters and exports

### Budget Management Application
**Independent Project | Flutter · Firebase**

Development of an application to create, organize and manage business quotations.

Main features:
- Client and quotation management
- Automatic calculations, including VAT
- Photo attachments and observations
- PDF document generation
- Quotation status tracking
